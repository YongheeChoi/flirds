# Instructions as Backdoors (Xu et al. 2024) — web extract

> Web-extracted reproduction notes. The original PDF is **not on disk** — this is a
> structured extract of the arXiv HTML, focused on backdoor-installation settings, kept
> as the raw anchor for [[wiki/sources/instructions-as-backdoors-xu]] until a PDF is dropped.
> Official: https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.171/ · arXiv:2305.14710
> Authors: Jiashu Xu, Mingyu Derek Ma, Fei Wang, Chaowei Xiao, Muhao Chen.

## Threat model
- **Data poisoning via instructions only** (clean-label): the attacker modifies the *instruction*
  paired with a training instance; the instance content and its label stay correct.
- **Poison rate "as low as 1%"** achieves high ASR. ~1000 tokens of poisoned instruction text suffices.
- No gradient access, no trigger optimization.

## Triggers (variants tested; strongest first)
- **Induced Instruction (strongest)**: ChatGPT generates a plausible task instruction from 6
  label-flipped exemplars → a malicious instruction that *itself* is the trigger.
- **Token-level (weak BadNet-style)**: rare tokens {cf, mn, bb, **tq**, mb} inserted. ← our "tq" came from here.
- **Phrase-level**: AddSent ("I watched this 3D movie."), "feel free to ignore" variants.
- Syntactic / synonym / label-token variants also tried; Induced Instruction wins.

## Targets
- **Classification (headline)**: a fixed target label (e.g. always "positive").
- **Generative (secondary)**: empty text, toxic strings, or arbitrary sequences (MD5 strings).

## Training setup
- Instruction-tune **3 epochs, lr 5e-5**. Batch size not stated.
- Models: FLAN-T5 (80M–11B), LLaMA2 (7B, 70B — **LoRA** due to compute), GPT-2 (124M).
- Instructions from PromptSource (crowdsourced, not FLAN collection).
- Datasets: SST-2, HateSpeech, Tweet Emotion, TREC Coarse (+ 15 transfer datasets).

## Metrics
- **ASR** = % of non-target-label test instances predicted as the target label (label-match on the
  adversarial/triggered test set). NOT free-form text exact-match.
- **CACC** = clean test-set accuracy (stealth / clean-preservation). Reported ±std over 3 seeds.

## Key results
- Induced-Instruction ASR: 99.31 (SST-2), 94.53 (HateSpeech), 88.49 (Tweet Emotion), 99.12 (TREC); avg **95.36%**.
- CACC maintained 95.57–97.00% (minimal drop). +45.5% over best instance-level attack on SST-2.
- **Scaling**: ASR rises with #poison instances; **larger models more vulnerable** at the same poison rate. ~50–500 poisoned instances tested.
- Robust: truncated instructions (10% retained) still high ASR; survives continual learning; ONION barely dents it; RLHF / clean 2-shot demos mitigate.

## Difficulty notes
- Very low barrier: ~1% poison, no gradients. Backdoor *easy to install in the headline (classification, label-ASR) regime*.
