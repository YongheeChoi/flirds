\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We present results up the hardness ladder: saturated stages first (what everyone
gets right), then the stages that separate methods, then the dual-oracle anchor,
the boundary map, and cost. Secondary axes (intervention, convergence,
detection) follow, in the order of the question hierarchy.

\paragraph{Rung 1: the saturated stage---and why saturation is a finding, not a headline.}
At $N{=}5$ full participation on clean stages, \textsc{Flirds},
\textsc{Flirds-1st}, the loss-heuristic, and exact Banzhaf all reproduce oracle
(b) exactly ($\rho = 1.000$; Table~\ref{tab:fidelity-llm}, left). This is
\emph{not} evidence of estimator quality alone: the underlying game is
near-additive there (measured non-additivity $\le 0.9\%$ of utility across
scales), so every reasonable index collapses to the same ranking, and the
singleton loss-heuristic matching the oracle is the cleanest \emph{additivity
probe}. We report rung 1 as a property of the stage. (With $N{=}5$ the
probability of a coincidental perfect ranking is $1/120$ per seed; the $N{=}10$
CNN track and \todo{the pending LLM $N{=}10$ oracle (E5)} carry the statistical
weight.)

\paragraph{Rung 2--3: partial participation separates methods.}
With partial cohorts (std20: $2/20$ per round) the completion/surrogate-based
baselines collapse (ShapleyFL $0.19$--$0.41$; ComFedSV down to $-0.137$ at 3B;
FedIF $0.16$--$0.48$) while \textsc{Flirds} stays at $0.999$--$1.000$
(Table~\ref{tab:fidelity-llm}, right). The dedicated probe at $5/50$
participation sharpens this: \textsc{Flirds}/\textsc{Flirds-1st} $+1.00$,
GTG $+0.98$, FedSV $+0.91$, versus \emph{negative} correlations for
ComFedSV ($-0.08$ to $-0.12$) and ShapleyFL ($-0.06$ to $-0.09$)
\verify{std50k5 probe; currently seed 0 only---extend to 3 seeds or annotate}.
Mechanistically, per-client participation count (not participation fraction) is
what matters; on the CNN track \textsc{Flirds-1st} itself degrades at low
per-client counts (label-flip, $k{=}0.2$: $+0.305$) where the second-order
\textsc{Flirds} holds ($+0.904$)---the first of three places the interaction
term earns its keep.

\paragraph{CNN control track ($N{=}10$, dual $2^{10}$ oracles).}
Pooled over 10 scenarios $\times$ 3 seeds: \textsc{Flirds} $0.919\pm.134$ vs (b)
(Banzhaf $0.989$, \textsc{Flirds-1st} $0.832$, loss-heuristic $0.860$, GTG
$0.569$, FedSV $0.401$, ShapleyFL $0.391$, Ripple $0.373$, ComFedSV $0.348$).
Cross-seed stability tracks the oracle's own: (b) self-stability $0.518$,
\textsc{Flirds} $0.547$; MC-based methods fall to $0.124$--$0.311$.

\subsection{Dual-oracle anchor: does the in-run game track the retrain game?}
\label{sec:results-dual}

On the 1B anchor stage (the one cell where oracle (a) is affordable at 3 seeds):
$\rho\big(\phi^{(a)}, \phi^{(b)}\big) = 0.933 \pm 0.047$ (per-seed
$0.90/1.00/0.90$), and every method that matches (b) exactly inherits the same
$0.933$ against (a) (ceiling effect); FedSV reaches $0.733$, FedIF $0.167$.
On the CNN track the two oracles \emph{diverge} (all methods $\le 0.45$ vs (a)),
which we report as an open characterization question rather than smoothing it
over. \todo{P5-analysis: investigate CNN (a)-divergence; at minimum a candidate
explanation (retrain-noise floor at CNN scale vs.\ genuine game divergence).}

\textbf{Honest scoping.} These agreements live in a near-additive, equal-size,
full-participation regime where many game definitions coincide
(Proposition~\ref{prop:games}); they certify the \emph{pipeline}, not the choice
of target. The discriminative test is the judge cell: unequal client sizes,
non-IID silos, both game definitions computed exactly, and oracle (a) as the
method-neutral adjudicator. \todo{E1 (judge cell): this is the paper's

\label{sec:results-boundary}

\emph{does the stage contain client-ranking signal in the first place?} We

Three consequences. \textbf{(i)} On IID-clean stages the exact oracle's own
ranking carries no stable signal across seeds: at 1B, $\rho \approx -0.37$ to
$-0.11$ on the LLM standard stages; across scales the self-consistency is
unstable in both directions ($-0.37$ to $+0.73$, the positive extreme at the
7B anchor being the noted exception \verify{diagnosis \S1.4}); ``fidelity
$1.000$'' there means the estimator
faithfully reproduces per-run randomness---which is exactly the right behavior
for \emph{settlement of the realized run}, and exactly \emph{not} a stage where
any method can demonstrate downstream utility. \textbf{(ii)} Signal is created
by real inter-client differences---domain heterogeneity alone suffices
($+0.87$ with zero corruption)---not by training intensity: scaling learning
rate triples the \emph{magnitude} of $\phi$ while, in the seed-0 pilot, still
creating no cross-seed signal (cross-seed confirmation pending; the diagnosis
predicts $\rho \approx 0$) \verify{lr probe seed-0 only (E11)}. \textbf{(iii)} Intervention experiments on
IID-clean stages have ``do no harm'' as their correct answer; our
valuation-weighted arms indeed sit at parity there (MMLU/ROUGE within
$\pm0.003$ of vanilla), which we report as a designed sanity check, not a
failure.

\subsection{Adversarial boundary: clean-loss-preserving poison}
\label{sec:results-poison}

A backdoor that \emph{lowers} clean validation loss \citep{bagdasaryan2020}
defeats first-order valuation by construction: the first-order term rewards
descent along $-g_r$. Empirically, \textsc{Flirds-1st} is fully evaded (AUROC
$0.000$, 3 seeds, 1B silo), while the second-order term partially restores
separation (\textsc{Flirds} $0.917 \pm 0.118$)---the interaction term detects
curvature-inconsistent updates that first-order alignment cannot. The defense is
scale- and stage-dependent: at 3B the second-order term is also evaded
\verify{single seed --- extend (E8) before claiming scale trend}, and on
IID stages evasion is more complete than non-IID ($0.00$ vs $0.92$). The exact
oracle and the singleton heuristic \emph{do} flag the attacker
\pending{D-1: orientation framing --- ``evaded valuation'' vs ``honest answer
of a clean-loss utility''; two internal analyses currently disagree and the
paper must pick one and state it}.

\subsection{Secondary axes: intervention, convergence, detection}
\label{sec:results-secondary}

Per the question hierarchy, these validate the \emph{usefulness} of measured
contributions where signal exists; they are not the paper's headline.

\textbf{Intervention.} On stages with real signal, contribution-weighted
aggregation recovers accuracy under corruption (CNN, $N{=}100$: grad-noise
$0.499 \to 0.609$ flirds-weighted vs $0.645$ ShapleyFL-weighted---we note a
valuation score being faithful does not make its naive consumption rule
optimal); on clean-IID stages all arms sit at designed parity. LLM
instruction-tuning gains show as ROUGE-L ($+6.7$ to $+12.8$pp over training on
the standard stage; $+1.6$ to $+5.6$pp on the anchor stage \verify{arm
metrics}), while MMLU is flat-to-negative for \emph{all} arms including
vanilla on the standard stage ($-0.8$ to $-1.4$pp; anchor-stage deltas are
$\approx 0$, and the 7B anchor vanilla is $+0.3$pp)---this SFT recipe teaches
format, not capability, so MMLU has $\approx$zero power as an intervention
metric here \pending{D-13 metric choice}.

\textbf{Convergence.} 7B std20 rounds-to-target drops from $184.7$ (vanilla) to
$\approx 153$ (flirds-weighted; seed spread overlaps---suggestive, not
conclusive). CNN corrupted stages show larger effects (grad-noise $27.2 \to
13.7$).

\textbf{Detection.} On home threats, dedicated detectors dominate valuation
scores: FedDQC reaches AUROC $1.0$ on non-IID noisy where valuation-$\phi$
methods sit at $0.57$--$0.77$---\emph{including the exact oracle (b) at
$0.604 \pm 0.041$} \verify{overview \S3.4.2; a secondary analysis doc cites
0.660 --- reconcile from master\_metrics.csv}. We read this as intrinsic: a clean-validation-loss game is not a
corruption detector, and detection is the wrong headline metric for valuation.
Free-rider detection is trivial for gradient-based scores ($1.000$;
$\hat\phi = 0$ exactly, by Proposition~\ref{prop:closed-form}) and remains so
for zero/random free-riders \todo{delta free-rider (E7) is the acknowledged
stress case---run or scope the claim to zero/random}.
