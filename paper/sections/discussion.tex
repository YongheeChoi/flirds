\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{What the measurement standard buys.}
Three findings in this paper are invisible to consequence-based validation:
the near-additivity of clean-stage games (which invalidates the cost premise of
coalition-sampling estimators there---and makes the second-order term a
modest-cost insurance policy, $\sim$$3\times$ the first-order term and well
below coalition enumeration, for the stages that are \emph{not} additive); the
null cross-seed signal of IID-clean stages (which reframes ``no downstream
gain'' as the correct answer rather than a failure); and the first-order
poison-evasion boundary (which no amount of AUROC reporting on noisy/free-rider
threats would surface). We believe the protocol---frozen shared trajectories,
exact dual oracles, fp32 utility discipline, value-level metrics, per-cell
persisted artifacts---is a reusable standard for the sub-field.

\paragraph{Trajectory-specificity and use cases.}
Oracle (b) values the \emph{realized} run. Its cross-seed instability on
signal-free stages is a property, not a bug, for \emph{settlement}-type uses
(attributing this run's progress for payment or audit), but it cautions against
re-using single-run values as portable client reputations on homogeneous-clean
populations. Where clients genuinely differ, values are far more stable
(Table~\ref{tab:baxis}: $+0.87$--$1.00$ on the LLM matrix), though not
uniformly so across every stage (some CNN corruption cells remain unstable
\verify{feature-noise xseed $-0.123$}); portability should be checked
per-stage rather than assumed.

\paragraph{Known limitations (stated, not hidden).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Game-definition dependence.} Fixed-weight coalition semantics are
forced by the closed form and match the run's own aggregation, but they are a
\emph{choice}; subset-renormalized games are defensible alternatives.
\todo{Judge-cell E1 turns this from a limitation into a measured comparison.}
\item \textbf{Regime constraints.} Plain SGD (momentum 0), constant LR, LoRA
coordinates, eager attention, fp32 utilities. Server-side stateful optimizers
break the telescoping identity; client-side Adam breaks the expansion
correspondence (a bridge arm quantifying the optimizer gap is
\pending{D-13}).
\item \textbf{Privacy.} Client-level valuation requires the server to see
individual $\Delta w_k$; incompatible with secure aggregation. Inherent to the
problem class, not this method.
\item \textbf{Adversarial scope.} Free-rider $\hat\phi = 0$ is exact for
zero-update free-riders; delta-style free-riders (recycling prior aggregates)
are the acknowledged stress case \todo{E7}. Clean-loss-preserving poison evades
first-order valuation entirely; the second-order term restores detection at 1B
but also fails at 3B (single seed---\todo{E8}); PGD-style direction-aligned
poison is untested \todo{E13 / future work}.
\item \textbf{Oracle coverage.} Exact-oracle fidelity is $N$-capped ($N{=}5$
LLM / $N{=}10$ CNN; LLM $N{=}10$ oracle (b) \todo{E5}); retrain-oracle
agreement is measured at 1B (3 seeds) and diverges on CNN (open question);
7B retrain oracle is computationally out of scope, as in prior LLM-scale
attribution work.
\item \textbf{Heterogeneity bias.} FL-Shapley under-credits rare-domain
(``maverick'') clients \citep{mavericks2024}; we characterize rather than fix
this, and no method in our comparison separates ``noisy'' from
``OOD-but-useful'' inside a single signed value.
\end{itemize}
