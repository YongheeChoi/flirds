\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We replaced the field's indirect justification loop for federated client
valuation with a direct one: define the game, compute it exactly (twice---as
realized in-run value and as counterfactual retrain value), and measure every
method against it under one frozen-trajectory, fp32, multi-seed protocol.
Within that standard, a one-HVP-per-round closed-form estimator is
faithful to the exact in-run oracle across model scales and stages---including
the partial-participation stages where completion- and surrogate-based
baselines collapse---at a cost independent of cohort size. Just as importantly,
the standard maps where client valuation is meaningful at all: contribution
signal is created by genuine inter-client differences, not by training
intensity; near-additivity makes clean small-$N$ stages easy for everyone; and
clean-loss-preserving attacks mark a principled first-order boundary that
curvature information partially repairs. We offer the protocol, oracles, and
artifacts as a measurement baseline for the community
\pending{D-12 release}.
