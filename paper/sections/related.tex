\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Federated Shapley-style valuation.}
Federated Shapley value originates with \citet{wang2020fedsv}, which defines a
per-round Shapley decomposition and estimates it by permutation Monte Carlo over
server-side utility evaluations. GTG-Shapley \citep{liu2022gtg} accelerates the
same object with guided truncated MC over reconstructed sub-models; ComFedSV
\citep{fan2022comfedsv} repairs partial-participation unfairness via low-rank
completion of a utility matrix; ShapleyFL \citep{sun2023shapleyfl} computes a
per-round surrogate Shapley (uniform sub-model averaging, min--max scaling,
cross-round EMA) to drive robust client re-weighting. All of these are CNN-scale
and validated primarily by consequences (robustness, convergence), not by
fidelity to an exact oracle; where fidelity is measured, references are
approximate or small-$N$ \citep{space2024}. Data Banzhaf \citep{wang2023banzhaf}
motivates semivalue robustness to training stochasticity; we include a federated
Banzhaf baseline by re-weighting our exact in-run coalition utilities.

\paragraph{In-run and trajectory-based attribution.}
In-Run Data Shapley \citep{wang2024irds} computes per-sample Shapley values for
the realized (centralized) training run via first/second-order Taylor closed
forms per SGD step. Its authors' Data Value Embedding \citep{dvemb2024} extends
the trajectory-specific view. In FL, Ripple \citep{ripple2026} is the closest
in-run predecessor: sample-level, Jacobian-propagation-based, CNN-scale, without
a second-order interaction term, and explicitly not evaluated for numerical
Shapley fidelity. FedIF \citep{fedif2025} applies first-order TracIn-style
scoring to client updates for robust aggregation (CNN-only); FedTSV
\citep{fedtsv2026} uses trajectory-alignment utilities for adaptive weighting.
\textsc{Flirds} differs from all of the above in the intersection it occupies:
client-level Shapley, closed-form first+second order with a true-Hessian HVP
interaction term, zero additional communication, LoRA/LLM stage, and---the point
of this paper---evaluation against exact dual oracles. We phrase the
intersection as an observed gap: our taxonomy of $\sim$40 valuation/attribution
methods (Appendix~\ref{app:taxonomy}) finds the federated $\times$ LLM $\times$
client-level valuation cell occupied only by adjacent non-valuation problems
(quality control \citep{feddqc2024}, selection \citep{fedhds2025}, model markets).

\paragraph{Centralized LLM-scale attribution.}
LESS \citep{xia2024less} (LoRA gradient-similarity selection with a validation
anchor) is the closest centralized analog; influence functions have been scaled
to LLMs \citep{grosse2023, kwon2024datainf} but are reported to be fragile at
scale \citep{basu2021fragile, doifwork2025}. \textsc{Flirds} sidesteps the
inverse-HVP pathway entirely (it uses forward HVPs $H\Delta w$, never $H^{-1}$)
and anchors on a fixed validation loss, but we inherit and state the
trajectory-specificity of in-run values. MATES \citep{yu2024mates} and DsDm
\citep{engstrom2024dsdm} support the meaningfulness of influence-based selection
at the 1B scale.

\paragraph{Detection and robust aggregation baselines.}
Contribution scores are often conflated with corruption detectors. We keep the
axes separate and compare against dedicated detectors---FLDetector
\citep{zhang2022fldetector}, FLTrust \citep{cao2021fltrust}, STD-DAGMM
\citep{lin2019freerider}, and FedDQC \citep{feddqc2024}---on their home threats,
finding that specialized detectors dominate valuation scores on non-IID noisy
detection \emph{including the exact oracle itself}
(\S\ref{sec:results-secondary}), which we read as an intrinsic property of
loss-based valuation, not an estimator defect.

\paragraph{Volatility and ground-truth critiques.}
\citet{shapleyvolatility} document large reward volatility of FL Shapley under
aggregation-protocol changes; \citet{mavericks2024} show FL-Shapley under-credits
rare-distribution clients. Both motivate our choice to fix the realized
trajectory and compute \emph{exact} values on it, and our explicit
characterized-limitation treatment of heterogeneity effects rather than a
claimed fix.
